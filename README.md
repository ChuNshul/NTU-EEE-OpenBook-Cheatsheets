## English

A curated collection of my personal open-book exam cheatsheets for **Digital Signal Processing (DSP)** and **Data Mining / AI**. 

These cheatsheets are highly condensed and carefully formatted. I personally used them during my finals and they saved me from the "flipping through hundreds of pages" nightmare.

### 📄 Included Files
*   **`dsp考试用第三版.docx`**: Highly refined 3rd version of DSP. Includes core formulas, pipeline architectures, hardware implementations, and assembly instructions. Extremely compact layout.
*   **`数据挖掘第一版.docx`**: The 1st version of Data Mining. Condensed core algorithms, key concepts, and must-know problem types.

### ✨ Features
*   **Battle-tested**: Used by the author in actual exams, focusing strictly on key points.
*   **Compact & Visual**: Dense layout with diagrams, perfect for **A4 double-sided printing**. Font size is just right for quick flipping.
*   **Highlighted**: Color-coded for easy spotting of key formulas and common mistakes.
*   **Editable**: Source Word (`.docx`) files are provided so you can customize them based on your own revision needs.

### 🚀 How to Use
1.  **Direct Download**: Click on the `.docx` files above, download them, and export/print to PDF (or print directly).
2.  **Modify**: Open the files to add your own notes. (Tip: Be careful with margins and font sizes when editing to keep it within A4 limits).

### ⚠️ Disclaimer
*   For study and revision purposes only. **Please do not use for commercial purposes.**
*   Exam policies may change. Please double-check with your professor regarding the allowed cheatsheet format.
